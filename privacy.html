Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This page explains how the Crypto Daan website handles information. Crypto Daan is a community-support campaign concept and this website is currently in pre-launch status.

Information you provide

If you contact the project through GitHub Issues, the information you submit is handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy. Please do not post private keys, seed phrases, passwords, financial account details or other sensitive personal information.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This website may use cookies or similar technologies if analytics or advertising services are enabled in the future. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve, measure and personalize ads as permitted by applicable settings and law. Visitors can review Google's advertising and privacy controls. No advertising service is currently promised to be active merely by reading this policy.

Third-party services

Links to GitHub and other external services are governed by those providers' own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their data practices.

Blockchain information

Public blockchain transactions are generally visible on public networks and may be difficult or impossible to reverse or erase. Do not publish personal information in transaction data.

Children

This website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the website changes. For questions, use the project's GitHub issue page.

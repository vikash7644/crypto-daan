About Crypto Daan

Crypto Daan is an independent community-support campaign concept focused on helping build open technology and developer tools.

What we work toward

QORVYNT Lang: an independent programming language, compiler, virtual machine and developer tooling.

QORVYNT Web4: research and development around agent coordination, decentralized identity, permissions and user-controlled data.

Developer ecosystem: SDKs, examples, documentation, testing and practical applications.

Transparency

The campaign is currently pre-launch. Its one-billion-people, one-dollar message is an aspiration, not a claim about funds raised or a guaranteed outcome. Contributions are not open. Wallet addresses shown on the website are owner-supplied and have not been independently verified.

Voluntary contributions, if and when accepted, do not purchase equity, ownership, tokens, repayment, rewards or financial returns. Crypto Daan is not represented as a registered charity or tax-exempt organization.
